# Ace Attorney Cut-In Creator
A web tool developed by ocarinaofdread.

## How to Use
1. Select your preferred file type. Please have patience while Unity loads!
   - **WebP** animations are smaller in file-size and support smooth transparency and millions of colors.
   - **GIF** animations are larger and include less animations, but upscale far better for something like DS animations.
2. Select a **style** from the "Style" dropdown.
3. Choose your preferred animation **FPS and resolution** (720p + 30fps is recommended for HD, and 768p + 30fps is recommended for DS).
4. Select whether you'd like to include **Spoiler sprites** in the cut-in dropdowns. Use this feature at your own discretion.
5. **Select your cut-ins!** You can choose from:
   - Preset Cut-Ins
   - Custom Cut-Ins (upload a .png image file into the area below the dropdown you'd like to replace)
       - Note: Your custom cut-in will disappear upon selecting a preset cut-in from the dropdown. If needed, you may download the cut-in template for reference.
6. Each style has its own custom properties that you can mess with, located below the Template button.
7. Select either **'Preview'** to preview the animation in your browser, or **'Render'** to render the animation!
   - Note: Loading the necessary plugin and rendering the animation may take a decent bit of time, depending on your browser, device, and internet speeds. Please be patient.
